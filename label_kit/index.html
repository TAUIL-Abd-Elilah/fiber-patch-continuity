How to judge a card

Each card shows one human-traced fiber and one candidate patch in six CT cross-sections of the PHercParis4 scan (20260411134726, 4.8 um voxels, 720 um across). Every section is cut perpendicular to the fiber at evenly spaced points along the stretch where the patch reaches it; "up" points toward the scroll's centre.

  • Yellow circle: the fiber in that section.
  • Cyan dots: where the candidate patch cuts the section. A panel without cyan means the patch does not reach that spot; ignore it.

Same sheet: in every panel with cyan, the cyan follows the papyrus sheet the fiber sits on. Different sheet: in at least one panel the cyan follows a neighbouring sheet, or the fiber is not on the sheet the cyan follows. Can't tell: the CT does not let you decide. Judge from the image only; the page does not know which answer any method gave. Keys: 1, 2, 3 to answer; arrow keys to move.
